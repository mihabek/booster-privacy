# Booster — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

Booster is a timer for short medication courses. It is made by MM Forge, a
small engineering team from Estonia, and published on Google Play under the
package name `com.mmforge.booster`.

## What Booster stores

Everything you enter — your schedule, the times you record a dose, the
optional medication name and milligram amounts, and your finished courses —
is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. Booster has
no account system, no server, and no analytics.

## What Booster sends

Nothing. Booster does not transmit any data off your device. It does not use
the internet at all. If you choose **Share** on a saved log, the app hands an
image of that log to the Android share sheet, and what happens to it from
there is up to you and the app you pick.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** and **Alarms & reminders** — only used, and only asked for,
  when you switch reminders on in the menu, so that the app can tell you when
  the next dose is due. They are off by default.
- **Receive boot completed** — so a pending reminder survives a restart.
- **Vibrate** — the haptic tap when a dose is recorded.

## Deleting your data

Delete any saved course from **History**, or clear all of them with
**Clear**. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

Booster is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any
information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same
address with an updated date.
